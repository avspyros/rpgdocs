Elf PCs are rare individuals gifted in both martial skill and arcane talent. Some are elves who have chosen to leave their hidden communities and walk among humankind, while others are half-elves
whose difficult lives have driven them to master both sword and spell in order to survive. They use the same spell lists and spell progressions as Magic-Users.

Elves’ enhanced senses allow them to Search more effectively than others (2 in 6 chance), and they are less often surprised than other races (1 in 6 chance).

<Aside type="note" title="Optional rule— Innate Magic" icon="pencil">
  To reflect an elf’s natural affinity for magic, you may use the following optional rule:

Elves do not require a spellbook to memorize spells. Instead of choosing a spell when they gain a level, they automatically learn one new spell of their choice each time they advance. However, each
spell learned this way can only be cast once per day.

Elves begin play at 1st level with the Read Magic spell.

</Aside>

### Details

Elves are creatures of magic related to the faerie. As such, they typically enjoy living in the deep wilderness and adapting their homes to be in harmony with their surroundings. They are on average
about five feet tall, and usually slender. Their most striking features are their pointed ears (how large these ears are will differ from region to region) and disproportionately large eyes which are
often a color not found in the other races.

Unlike the Dwarfs, the Elves recognize that the expansive nature of Humans is simply the way of things, and accept their decline with grace. Their attitude towards Humans is not so unified, however.
Some see them as the natural heir to creation and seek to guide them and help them rule in wisdom. Some see Man as a great blight, and work to destroy them before he destroys all natural order. And
some, considered young and foolish by their people, walk among Man.
