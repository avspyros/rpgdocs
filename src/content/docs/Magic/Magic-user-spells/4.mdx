<Spell SpellTitle="Charm Monster" SpellDuration="See Below" SpellRange="120'">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Makes one large creature or several smaller creatures enraptured by the caster if they fail a save vs. Magic.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Affects 1 target with more than 4 Hit Dice, or 3d6 targets with 4 or fewer Hit Dice (undead are immune). The target is not mind-controlled, but retains its personality while viewing the caster
    with absolute fascination and a desire to please.
  </p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Requests against the target's nature or interests trigger a save vs. Magic to refuse (success refuses the order without breaking the spell). Promising appropriate rewards bypasses this save.
  </p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Abuse, neglect, or violent acts trigger new saving throws to break the spell entirely (murderous violence grants +5 to the save). Intelligent targets remember everything once the spell ends.
  </p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Confusion" SpellDuration="1 Round/level" SpellRange="120'">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Causes living creatures in a 15' radius area to act randomly each round on a failed save vs. Magic.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Affects living creatures in a 15' radius. Targets that fail a saving throw vs. Magic cannot act normally and must roll 1d6 on their initiative count each round. See table below
  </p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    If an indicated action cannot be performed, the target babbles incoherently instead. Attacked targets automatically retaliate against their attacker on their next turn, ignoring the roll result.
  </p>
</Spell>

1. Attacks caster or caster’s allies
2. Acts normally
3. Babbles incoherently
4. Meanders away for 1 Turn
5. Attacks nearest creature
6. Attacks own allies.

<Spell SpellTitle="Creation, Minor" SpellDuration="Instantaneous" SpellRange="Touch">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Creates a nonliving vegetable matter object up to 1 cubic foot per caster level.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Fabricates an item made of plant-based material (e.g., rope, wood, cloth, basket) up to a maximum volume of 1 cubic foot per caster level.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Requires a tiny piece of the same material as a spell component (e.g., a splinter of wood to create a door, a piece of straw for a basket).</p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Dig" SpellDuration="Instantaneous" SpellRange="30'">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Excavates earth, sand, or mud in 10' cube increments, leaving pits or trenches.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Digs soil, mud, or sand in 10' cube increments, depositing the earth around the perimeter. Digging straight down risks wall collapse per 5' depth (Soil: 15% cumulative, Sand: 35% cumulative, Mud:
    55% cumulative).
  </p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Creatures passing within 1' of an opening must save vs. Paralyzation to avoid falling in. Pits dug directly under a stationary target fall in automatically, while fast-moving targets get a save
    vs. Magic to avoid falling.
  </p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Dimension Door" SpellDuration="Instantaneous" SpellRange="10'">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Instantly teleports a target to a precise known or relative location within 360'.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Transfers the target to any exact spot desired within 360'. Unwilling targets are granted a save vs. Magic to resist.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Destination can be specified by relative direction/distance (e.g., 100' south, 20' up) without line of sight. If the destination is occupied by a solid object, the spell fails.
  </p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Extension" SpellDuration="See Below" SpellRange="0">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Extends the duration of an active spell previously cast by the caster by 50%.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Increases the remaining and total duration of a currently active spell cast by the same Magic-User by 50%.</p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Globe of Invulnerability, Minor" SpellDuration="1 Round/level" SpellRange="0">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Creates a 10' diameter immobile sphere that blocks all 1st–3rd level Magic-User spell effects.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Forms an immobile 10' sphere centered on the caster that completely prevents 1st, 2nd, and 3rd level Magic-User spells from affecting anyone inside. Spells can still be cast outward from inside
    the globe.
  </p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Spells of 4th level or higher, as well as spells already active before casting, are unaffected. The sphere can be dispelled by Dispel Magic.</p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Hallucinatory Terrain" SpellDuration="Permanent" SpellRange="240'">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Alters the visual, auditory, and olfactory appearance of an area of terrain.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Disguises terrain to look, sound, and smell like a different type of terrain across an area of up to one 10' x 10' square per caster level.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">The entire area to be disguised must fit completely within the spell's 240' range limit.</p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Invisibility, Improved" SpellDuration="1 Turn/level" SpellRange="Touch">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Renders a target completely invisible, allowing them to attack without breaking the effect.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Makes a target and their carried equipment completely invisible. Dropped items become visible, and picked-up items vanish. Carried light sources remain visible only to the subject.
  </p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">The subject still produces sound, scent, and physical mass. Unlike standard Invisibility, taking aggressive or offensive actions does not break the spell.</p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Mnemonic Enhancer" SpellDuration="Instantaneous" SpellRange="0">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Allows the caster to prepare or retain up to 3 total levels of extra spells.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Grants up to 3 additional spell levels (e.g., three 1st-level spells, one 3rd-level spell) beyond the caster's normal daily maximum.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Alternatively, casting this prior to casting other spells allows the Magic-User to discharge up to 3 spell levels while retaining them in memory for one additional casting.
  </p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Plant Growth" SpellDuration="Permanent" SpellRange="120'">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Causes normal vegetation to become a dense thicket, drastically slowing movement.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Causes existing vegetation across a customizable area (up to a 20' square per caster level) to grow into an impassable thicket.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Movement speed through the affected area drops to 10' (or 20' for large creatures). Requires natural brush or trees to cast; has no effect on plant creatures.</p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Polymorph Others" SpellDuration="Permanent" SpellRange="60'">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Transforms a living creature into another type of being permanently unless dispelled.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Transforms one target into a different creature (unwilling targets receive a save vs. Magic). The target retains its original Hit Points, but acquires all physical and mental traits of the new
    form (including Hit Dice limits up to twice original HD, Intelligence, instincts, and alignment).
  </p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Cannot copy a specific individual's appearance. The effect is negated by Dispel Magic, and the subject reverts to its natural form upon death.</p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Polymorph Self" SpellDuration="1 Turn/level" SpellRange="0">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Transforms the caster into a typical member of another creature species.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Allows the caster to assume the form of any creature with Hit Dice equal to or less than their own. Cannot imitate a specific individual.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Retains original Intelligence, Hit Points, saving throws, and attack capability while gaining the physical attributes and attacks (e.g., flight, Strength, natural weapons) of the new form. Magical
    and special abilities (e.g., gaze attacks) are not gained, and spellcasting is disabled while transformed.
  </p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Can be ended by Dispel Magic. The caster reverts to their natural form upon death.</p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Protection from Normal Weapons" SpellDuration="1 Round/level" SpellRange="Touch">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Grants complete immunity to non-magical weapons.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Makes the target immune to all non-magical weapon attacks.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Monsters with 4 Hit Dice or greater (excluding character levels) can still harm the target with non-weapon natural attacks. Offers no protection against spells or magical effects.
  </p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Seven Gates" SpellDuration="4d6 Turns" SpellRange="See Below">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Activates seven pre-marked gates within a 5-mile radius for dimensional travel.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Activates seven pre-prepared gates marked at designated locations up to 5 miles apart. The first gate forms directly in front of the caster.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Entering a gate requires rolling a 1d8 to determine the exit gate. A result of 8 traps the traveler between dimensions for 1d6 Turns; afterwards, they may roll a save vs. Magic each Turn to emerge
    at a random gate before the spell duration expires.
  </p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Shadow Monsters" SpellDuration="1 Round/level" SpellRange="30'">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Creates semi-real illusions of monsters in a 20' square area that deal real or reduced damage.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Summons pseudo-real monsters of a single type within a 20' x 20' area, up to a total HD equal to the caster's level. Shadow monsters have 2 Hit Points per normal Hit Die.
  </p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Targets receive a save vs. Magic. If failed, the monsters attack and deal full damage as normal for their creature type. If successful, targets realize their nature, treating the monsters as AC 12
    and taking only one-quarter normal damage.
  </p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Speak with Plants" SpellDuration="1 Round/level" SpellRange="0">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Enables communication with normal plants and plant creatures, and can persuade them to clear paths.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Allows the caster to ask questions and receive answers from both normal vegetation and plant creatures.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Plants can be asked to move to clear impassable or overgrown paths. Does not automatically make plant creatures friendly, though friendly ones may perform favors or services.
  </p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Wall of Fire" SpellDuration="1 Round/level" SpellRange="80' or 0">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Creates a wall or moving ring of fire that damages creatures passing through or standing nearby.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    Creates a flaming barrier (either a stationary wall up to 20' square per level at 80' range, or a 20' high ring with a radius up to 5' per level centered on and moving with the caster).
  </p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">
    One side emits searing heat; creatures within 10' of the hot side take 1d6 damage. Passing through the flames deals 2d6 damage. Undead take double damage from all effects of the wall.
  </p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Persists for as long as the caster concentrates, or lasts for 1 Round per level once concentration ends.</p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Wall of Ice" SpellDuration="12 Turns" SpellRange="120'">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Creates a customizable, translucent ice wall that blocks weak creatures and damages stronger ones breaking through.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Summons an immobile ice wall up to 1,200 square feet in any shape or dimension. Must rest on a solid surface and cannot be cast in spaces occupied by objects.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Creatures with less than 4 Hit Dice cannot pass through. Creatures with 4 or more Hit Dice suffer 1d6 damage when breaking through.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Deals double damage to fiery or heat-adapted creatures.</p>
</Spell>

<Spell SpellTitle="Wizard Eye" SpellDuration="1 Turn/level" SpellRange="0">
  <p slot="spellsummary">Creates an invisible floating sensor that provides darkvision and scouts ahead.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Summons an invisible magical sensor with 60' darkvision that sends visual information back to the caster.</p>
  <p slot="spelldescription">Moves at a rate of up to 120' per Turn in any direction. Blocked by solid barriers, but can pass through apertures as small as 1 inch in diameter.</p>
</Spell>
