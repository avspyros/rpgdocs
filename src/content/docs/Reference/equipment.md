- Items in italics are considered Non-Encumbering items for encumbrance purposes, although the GM can rule that quantities of the items do count towards encumbrance.
- Items in bold and italics are considered to be Oversized.
- A quiver plus the arrows or bolts it carries (20 maximum) count as one item together for [encumbrance](/reference/movement_encumbrance) purposes.
- If a cost in parenthesis exists, it refers to the different rural price. A dash means the item cannot be purchased in rural areas.
